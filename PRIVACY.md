# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

## Local processing

Codex Process Jobs runs on the user's macOS or Linux machine. Job metadata,
status, and bounded logs are stored locally under the user's Codex home. CPJ
does not operate a hosted job service and does not send process output, job
records, or usage telemetry to Filament Labs.

## Codex and third-party services

When Codex inspects a saved result, the selected bounded output may be processed
by OpenAI under the user's account and applicable OpenAI terms. Commands
launched by CPJ can access networks or third-party services when the user
authorizes them; those services have their own privacy practices.

## Environment and notifications

CPJ does not persist the launching process's environment. A launched command
still receives its inherited environment and may use it. Sanitized completion
notices contain validated job status information and do not include process
output.

## Website and support

The CPJ website does not set cookies, use a contact form, or include client-side
analytics. The hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata needed to
deliver the pages. If a user contacts support or opens a GitHub issue, Filament
Labs receives the information the user chooses to provide.

## Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to <info@filamentlabs.io>.
